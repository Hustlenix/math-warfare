# Credits and artwork rights

Project concept and original MATH-Warefare: Lalith / Hustlenix. This Numberglen expansion has substantial Codex AI implementation assistance. Do not describe its generated code as human-only or count AI execution as personal coding hours.

Pixel artwork: **LimeZu**, https://limezu.itch.io/

Used locally supplied paid packs:

- Modern Interiors full version: https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors — eleven premade characters, animated cat/frog/butterflies, furniture crops and the body, eyes, hairstyles, clothing and accessory layers used to prebuild the wardrobe. Direction frames are reordered to consistent left/up/right/down strips. Side-facing art is used directly, without mirroring a back frame.
- Fungus Cave: https://limezu.itch.io/fungus-cave — animated cave characters, stone/wall tiles, mushrooms, throne, camp furnishings and chest.
- RPG Fantasy Battlers complete version: https://limezu.itch.io/fantasy-battlers — four static battlers, animated in the game through code.

The other supplied folders include duplicate resolutions/formats and free versions. The Windows installer and Linux character-generator application were not needed or executed; existing PNG character sheets supplied the game characters. The original downloaded packs were not modified.

The 324 wardrobe looks are game-specific composites and colour variations of licensed LimeZu layers, with additional curly-hair, hat, goatee and bandana pixels authored in the offline build script with Codex assistance. Palette packing reduces the download size. Frontier scenery and the caravan are drawn in code. The user-supplied black-hat portrait informed the desert-chemist silhouette; the portrait file, television stills, film posters and franchise logos are not bundled. This is an independent fan-inspired visual tribute, with no claim of affiliation or endorsement.

The local Modern Interiors full-version licence permits use/editing in commercial and noncommercial projects, requires LimeZu credit, and forbids reselling or distributing the asset itself. The artist lists commercial licences for the paid Fungus Cave and complete Fantasy Battlers packs. Use the paid versions for this game, not the noncommercial free/trial versions.

Original packs and cropped images are excluded from public source archives and Git. The compiled playable game embeds only the crops used by this project. That does not grant anyone a licence to extract, redistribute, sell or reuse the artwork. Acquire your own pack licences to build from source. Public code access is not an artwork licence.

Pixel typeface: Press Start 2P, licensed under SIL Open Font License. See `public/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. Game sound is synthesised in code; no downloaded music or sound files are used.

The small four-direction walking cat poses are original pixels authored in `companion.js` with Codex assistance. LimeZu’s licensed curled-cat animation is used for its sleeping pose, at a small cropped display size. Frog animation remains LimeZu artwork; its movement and hops are implemented in code. Expanded farm, creek, bridges, barn, tents and companion shelter are code-drawn scenery, mixed with the credited furniture crops.
