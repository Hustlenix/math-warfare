# What is ready, and what still belongs to the owner

The previous Shipwright feedback asked for a distinctive interface, a complete README, and compliance with an indicated maximum of 30% AI use. Numberglen addresses the visual and documentation work with an explorable pixel world, contextual quests, paid artist assets and a documented setup.

**It does not establish compliance with the AI limit.** This expansion was substantially written by Codex. It must be disclosed. Acceptance, recertification, votes, eligible logged hours and Stardust are external decisions. No amount, including 300 Stardust, is guaranteed by this build.

Before requesting recertification:

1. Play the entire journey yourself and record real issues, decisions and changes you personally make.
2. Ask the organisers how this disclosed AI-assisted expansion affects the project’s eligibility. Do not conceal it or assert an unmeasured percentage.
3. Get independent players to try keyboard and touch controls, the first quest, the cave gates, and wrong-answer recovery. Record feedback honestly.
4. Link the current playable build and public source. Give LimeZu credit and retain artwork restrictions.
5. Write your own factual devlog based on actual work. Use genuine screenshots; do not invent hours, measurements, reviews, players or learning claims.

No Stardance devlog, time entry, recertification request, vote, or message to a reviewer is submitted by this build process.
